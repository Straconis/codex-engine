New page reader: click a search result to read the whole page, with Prev/Next, and switch between AI-formatted, cleaned and raw text.
Pages are cleaned up automatically at import: running headers, footers and page numbers are removed, headings are detected, paragraphs and lists are rebuilt, words split across lines are re-joined, and stat blocks get bold labels and an ability-score table. Search uses the cleaned text too.
Books imported with earlier versions are upgraded automatically the first time you open one in the reader.
Optional local AI formatting (through Ollama) restores structure such as headings, lists, quotes and tables. It never rewrites the book: any output that changes, drops or adds words is rejected and the cleaned text is kept. Text never leaves your computer.
New Settings panel: Codex Engine can run Ollama for you and store AI models in any folder, including a second or external drive, and can download models with a progress bar. Each computer keeps its own settings.
Everything works without Ollama; the reader simply shows the cleaned text.
