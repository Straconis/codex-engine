AI formatting is stricter about keeping the book's text exactly as written. It can no longer join or split real words ("can not" to "cannot", "therapist" to "the rapist"), remove fill-in blanks like "______", remove a footnote asterisk or the "#" in "item #3", or drop a number at the edge of a part of a page (0.3.10 could still drop a stat such as the "15" under "Armor Class"). Only a word split across a line break may be re-joined. A page that passed the old check but not this one shows the "Reformat page" notice, and "Check AI pages" lists them.
The AI's reply is cleaned up better when it wraps the page in a code block, so a page is never saved as one big code block. Short headings such as "Gear" can now be AI formatted, and the "what changed" summary is much faster on long pages.
Better page cleanup: a word like "MIMIC" or "I" near the top or bottom of a page is no longer mistaken for a page number; a list of short bold names (such as spell names) stays one heading per line; "Half-" at the end of a line followed by "Orc" becomes "Half-Orc", not "Half- Orc"; a sentence that wraps onto a line starting with "15." or a dash is no longer cut into a list item; two stat blocks in a row each get their own ability score table; and an asterisk in a heading shows correctly. Books are re-cleaned automatically the next time you open them; your own edits and AI versions are kept.
Search ignores quote marks, section breaks and italics markers in pages you edited.
