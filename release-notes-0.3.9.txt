New "Check AI pages" button on each book in the Sources list. It finds every page whose AI version fails the stricter check from 0.3.8, or was made from older cleaned text, and lists them so you can open each one.
"Reformat all" reformats those pages one after another, with the usual progress window. You can stop at any time. If the AI still can't format part of a page without changing the text, that page is listed for you to decide on in the reader; nothing is decided for you.
